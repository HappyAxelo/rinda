# Changes 25 November 2026: daily 20 m new-water outlook

## What users see

- **Overview** opens with a "Daily new-water outlook" card: how many places may get new water 7 to 14 days ahead,
  candidate cells per day, the radar pass it came from, and a link to the map. The older site-based forecast (598
  points, stale since 11 November) is folded under it.
- **Surveillance map** has a new first layer, "Daily new-water outlook", switched on by default; the stale site
  forecast layer now starts switched off.
  - One button per day (+7 to +14, with dates) or "All days" (highest chance over the window).
  - Plain legend: low chance to high chance of new water; optional "water seen on the latest pass" and "low, flat
    valley floor" overlays.
  - "Places to inspect first": the top eight candidates; selecting one zooms to it and shows its chance on each day.
  - "How good is this forecast?" states the test result in words, and that inland signals are not confirmed by
    Sentinel-2 and need a check on foot.
- Candidates are always labelled "Candidate: unverified". They are not breeding sites and authorise nothing.

## API

- `GET /api/v1/map/grid20`: outlook metadata, per-day summary, skill and candidate polygons (GeoJSON).
- `GET /api/v1/map/grid20/{d7..d14|max|habitat_prone|wet_now}.png`: Web Mercator overlays. Any other name is 404.
- Both need `read:forecast`. The offline app shell (`rinda-shell-v5`) keeps the latest outlook metadata.

## Data

`study/g20/` is written by `habitat-forecast/scripts/g20_daily.py` (about 360 KB). Method, results and the label check:
`habitat-forecast/outputs/g20/README.md` and `PROTOCOL.md`. If `study/g20/` is absent, the card and layer say that
the outlook has not been produced.
