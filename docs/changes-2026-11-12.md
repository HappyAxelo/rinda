# Changes 2026-11-12: research alignment and implementation report

This release builds on the 10 November rebuild (`docs/changes-2026-11-10.md`). It adds what the research brief asked
for beyond that rebuild. RINDA is still **deployable for controlled evaluation, not validated for intervention
decisions**.

## Audit of the live site (12 November)

- `https://rinda.onrender.com` was serving the 10 November rebuild (commit `efda68f`).
- CI on that commit: tests passed, Docker build and serve passed, **memory guard failed** at 451 MB against a limit of
  about 449 MB. The hosted service has 512 MB, so it ran with little headroom.
- This release lowers start-up and request memory (see below). The Linux figure is only known once CI runs.

## Memory

- Scene catalogues are read row by row at first start and skipped afterwards.
- The radar snapshot behind the Earth-observation layer no longer copies and sorts the whole series on each request.
- Memory freed during start-up is returned to the system on Linux.
- Windows peak working set on the CI request sequence plus the new endpoints: 351 MB on a fresh database (352 MB before).
  Windows cannot show the Linux saving; CI measures it.

## Implementation report

### Complete and tested
- Executive overview from records only, with action queue and data freshness.
- Surveillance map of the Gikomero pilot: forecast, Earth observation, sensors, entomology, weather, operations.
- Habitat dossier that keeps predicted, satellite-observed, sensor-observed, field-verified, confirmed, authorised and
  treated evidence apart.
- Historical data: dataset upload with quality report, confirmation and versions; Earth-observation inventory.
- Reproducible 7-day and 14-day training with temporal embargo, held-out blocks, baselines, calibration and a hash.
- Field workflow with offline queue, review, reasons, photographs.
- Treatment decisions that need verified larvae, a suitability assessment, a protocol reference and a supervisor.
- Drone missions: inspection from forecasts, treatment only from authorised habitats, approval, exports, results.
- Reporting in four groups with CSV and PDF; RE-AIM, weekly report, DHIS2 export.
- Seven roles enforced on the server; audit trail; backups.
- 72 automated tests.

### Partially implemented
- **Forecasting method.** One model family is served (gradient-boosted trees on site histories). Spatio-temporal deep
  models were not built: with 600 point samples and about 2,000 events a year they are not justified, and the audit
  shows the limiting factor is the label, not the algorithm.
- **Water classes.** Existing water and newly detected water come from radar passes; predicted water from the forecast.
  "Potential vector habitat" has no model, because there are no larval labels.
- **Kinyarwanda.** Draft for navigation and field screens only. French: slot only.
- **Photographs** are not kept offline.
- **Cell boundaries** are not bundled (licence). The cell filter is disabled.
- **Landsat and Sentinel-1 GRD** are catalogued, not processed.

### Needs external data, credentials or approval
- RBC entomological records and an entomology team (requested).
- Sensor nodes in the field; Meteo Rwanda access; Copernicus credentials and a raster worker for new acquisitions.
- NISR cell and village boundaries; an official marshland boundary.
- Approved national protocol settings; RBC role mapping; DHIS2 identifiers; an SMS provider.
- Drone pilot and flight authorisation; Bti supply and approval.

### Needs scientific validation
- **The forecast.** The 5 November audit stands: labels mostly reflect lake-shore water and an unharmonised satellite.
  Sentinel-1C must be corrected, labels rebuilt and the protocol rerun before any claim about marshland water.
- **H1** needs forecasts archived before their outcomes are known.
- **The habitat model (H5)**, transfer to a second area (H3), sensor calibration (H4), model selection by measured
  latency (H2) and the spray controller (H6) are untested.
- **Operational effect.** No field outcome has been recorded. RINDA reports activity, not malaria.

### Not checked
- The full journey was exercised by API tests and screen loads, not clicked end to end by hand.
- Linux memory is measured by CI after the push.

## Addendum, 13 November 2026: current forecast and lower memory

- **Forecasts now run to the latest radar pass.** Sentinel-1 RTC was extended to 11 November 2026, Sentinel-2 to 9 November,
  ERA5-Land and archived NWP to 12 November, CHIRPS to 5 November (`habitat-forecast/scripts/10_extract_pc.py`).
  `habitat-forecast/scripts/v3_issue_current.py` rebuilt the inputs with passes whose outcome is not yet known and scored
  them with the stored v3 models. Nothing was refitted and the evaluation was not touched. The rebuilt inputs matched the
  stored ones exactly on all 145,784 overlapping rows at 7 days.
- **Forward check of the frozen model** (`habitat-forecast/outputs/v3/forward_check_2026-10_11.json`): on 6,331 rows from
  5 October to 5 November 2026 with observed outcomes (226 events), 7-day PR-AUC was 0.785 against 0.508 for the
  recent-water rule; at 14 days, 0.675 against 0.352 (6,335 rows, 169 events). These forecasts were not archived before
  their outcomes and the labels still carry the 5 November audit findings, so this is not the H1 test.
- **Outlook** (`GET /outlook`, top of the overview): today's date, the latest issue for 7 and 14 days, counts by tier, the
  ECMWF rain forecast as retrieved, and what to inspect, decide, treat and follow up. Treatment advice comes only from field
  records; a forecast is never grounds for spraying.
- **Memory:** model inputs are memory-mapped, so a request reads only the rows it needs. Windows peak on the CI sequence:
  308 MB (388 MB before this change with the larger inputs).
- **Refreshing:** rerun the four extraction commands with a new `--end`, then `v3_issue_current.py --end <date>`, then
  commit `study/v3`. This is manual; no scheduled job exists.

## Addendum, 13 November 2026: Sentinel-1C correction (E1)

- The study corrected Sentinel-1C and 1D onto Sentinel-1A per site, rebuilt the labels and reran the v3 protocol
  (`habitat-forecast/outputs/v3_e1/`). Corrected PR-AUC: 0.599 vs 0.534 (7 d), 0.569 vs 0.484 (14 d). 95% of events are at
  the lake shore; inside the sector over 500 m from the lake there were 17 events and no skill.
- RINDA now says so: the audit banner carries a fourth finding and a new summary, the model registry lists the corrected
  evaluation as `v3-e1-h7` and `v3-e1-h14` (evaluated, not served), and the research-alignment status and data notes are updated.
- **Not changed:** the served models and forecasts are still the uncorrected v3. Approval of a Target A model stays blocked.
  The October and November forward check above used the uncorrected labels.
